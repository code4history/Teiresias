Notable changes to this project are recorded here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- 適合スコア（`score`）の分子と分母の尺度をそろえました（[#26](https://github.com/code4history/Teiresias/issues/26)）。これまでは分母が地上の距離（m）、分子が候補の CRS の座標での残差で、縮尺係数が 1 から離れた CRS ほどスコアが大きく出ていました（EPSG:3857 は緯度 φ で 1/cos φ 倍。日本では 1.2〜1.4 倍）。今は、GCP のある領域での候補の CRS の縮尺係数 `k` で残差を割り、地上の距離に直してから分母で割ります（`score = (basicRmsMeters / k) / L_geo`）。縮尺係数が 1 に近い候補（平面直角座標系・UTM）のスコアはほぼ変わりません（`k` は `L_geo` と同じ地上の近似〔球〕に対する比で、楕円体との差 0.3% 以内が入ります。実測で盛岡 +0.16%）。EPSG:3857 のスコアは 1/cos φ 倍だけ小さくなり、地図と地上がちょうど相似なら 3857 と平面直角座標系のスコアがほぼ等しくなります。実地図（古地図のデモ 3 枚）の「平面直角座標系 ÷ 3857」は 0.77〜0.83 から 0.998〜1.014 になりました。`ranked[].rmsMeters` と `selected.rmsMeters` は、従来どおり候補の座標での残差です。`ranked` の順は score に従います。
- The scale of the numerator and the denominator of the fit score (`score`) are now the same ([#26](https://github.com/code4history/Teiresias/issues/26)). Until now the denominator was a distance on the ground (m) but the numerator was the residual in the coordinates of the candidate CRS, so the score came out larger for a CRS whose scale factor is far from 1 (EPSG:3857 stretches by 1/cos φ at latitude φ: 1.2 to 1.4 times in Japan). The residual is now divided by the scale factor `k` of the candidate CRS in the area of the GCPs, which brings it back to a distance on the ground, before it is divided by the denominator (`score = (basicRmsMeters / k) / L_geo`). The score of a candidate with a scale factor near 1 (the Japan Plane Rectangular CS, UTM) hardly changes (`k` is a ratio to the same ground approximation as `L_geo`, a sphere, so the difference from the ellipsoid, within 0.3 %, enters it; +0.16 % measured at Morioka). The score of EPSG:3857 becomes smaller by 1/cos φ, and when the map and the ground are exactly similar the scores of 3857 and of a plane rectangular CS are nearly equal. On real maps (3 maps of the old-map demo) the ratio "plane rectangular CS / 3857" changed from 0.77–0.83 to 0.998–1.014. `ranked[].rmsMeters` and `selected.rmsMeters` are unchanged: the residual in the coordinates of the candidate. The order of `ranked` follows the score.
- デモの説明文から、正規化なし（`chirality: 'direct-only'`）で反射した GCP を当てると「真値が上位から落ちる」という記述を除きました。反射した GCP の当てはまりは崩れ、地上の距離で測ると候補どうしが同じ当てはまりになります。以前の「落ちる」順位は、候補の縮尺係数の差（相対 2e-4）が順位を決めていたものです。識別力の札が「潰れている」ことは変わりません。
- In the demo text, the statement that "the true CRS falls from the top" when reflected GCPs are fitted without normalization (`chirality: 'direct-only'`) was removed. The fit of reflected GCPs breaks down, and measured as distances on the ground the candidates fit equally badly. The earlier "falling" order was decided by the difference of the scale factors of the candidates (2e-4 relative). The label of the discrimination still says "collapsed".

### Added
- 初版。npm に `@c4h/teiresias` として公開します。主な API は `estimateCrs`（対応点と候補の CRS から、もっともよく当たる CRS とワールドファイルを推定する）と、その入出力の型（`CandidateCrs`・`CandidateTransformer`・`EstimateOptions`・`EstimationResult`・`EstimationFailure` ほか）です。全 export は README の公開 API の節にあります。候補と変換関数は `@c4h/atlas` の `listProjectedCrsFromProjWasm` と `createCandidateTransformer` で作れます。
- First release, published to npm as `@c4h/teiresias`. The main API is `estimateCrs` (estimates the CRS and world file that best fit the ground control points among the candidate CRSs) and the types of its input and output (`CandidateCrs`, `CandidateTransformer`, `EstimateOptions`, `EstimationResult`, `EstimationFailure` and others). Every export is listed in the Public API section of the README. The candidates and the transformer can be made with `listProjectedCrsFromProjWasm` and `createCandidateTransformer` of `@c4h/atlas`.
- 失敗の `message` は 500 文字で切った後、元の文面を保持しません（長い文面の失敗が多数あってもメモリが増え続けません）。
- A failure `message` no longer keeps the original text after it is cut to 500 characters (memory does not keep growing when many failures carry long texts).
- 変換の形の推奨 `recommendTransformSetup`（推定結果の `score` から、world file だけ・三角網＋大域モデル・三角網＋扇形のどれを勧めるか。境の近くでは 2 つ）と、`recommendTransformSetupFromScore`・`shouldRecommendCrsSwitch`・閾値の定数 `TRANSFORM_SETUP_THRESHOLDS`・`CRS_SWITCH_MAX_RATIO`。閾値は仮の値です。
- Transform-setup recommendation `recommendTransformSetup` (from the `score` of the estimation, which of world file only, triangulation + global model, or triangulation + fan to recommend; two near a border), together with `recommendTransformSetupFromScore`, `shouldRecommendCrsSwitch` and the threshold constants `TRANSFORM_SETUP_THRESHOLDS` and `CRS_SWITCH_MAX_RATIO`. The thresholds are provisional.
- 既知の制約は README の「[既知の制限](./README.ja.md#既知の制限)」にあります。
- Known limitations are described in "[Known limitations](./README.md#known-limitations)" in the README.
